### 1. When an opaque object placed in the path of light becomes extremely small, light exhibits a tendency to bend around its edges. What is this phenomenon called?
A. Total internal reflection  
B. Diffraction  
C. Dispersion  
D. Lateral displacement  
**Answer: B**  
*Rationale:* When an obstacle is comparable in size to the wavelength of light, rectilinear propagation fails and light bends around corners, an effect termed diffraction.

### 30. An object is placed $20\text{ cm}$ in front of a convex mirror of radius of curvature $30\text{ cm}$. What is the position of the image?
A. $+8.57\text{ cm}$ behind the mirror  
B. $-8.57\text{ cm}$ in front of the mirror  
C. $+12.0\text{ cm}$ behind the mirror  
D. $-60.0\text{ cm}$ in front of the mirror  
**Answer: A**  
*Rationale:* $R = +30\text{ cm} \implies f = +15\text{ cm}$, $u = -20\text{ cm}$.  
$$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{15} - \frac{1}{-20} = \frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{20} = \frac{4 + 3}{60} = \frac{7}{60} \implies v = +\frac{60}{7} \approx +8.57\text{ cm}$$

### 31. An object $4\text{ cm}$ tall is placed at $25\text{ cm}$ in front of a concave mirror of focal length $15\text{ cm}$. What is the image distance ($v$)?
A. $-15.0\text{ cm}$  
B. $-37.5\text{ cm}$  
C. $+37.5\text{ cm}$  
D. $-50.0\text{ cm}$  
**Answer: B**  
*Rationale:* $f = -15\text{ cm}$, $u = -25\text{ cm}$.  
$$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{-15} - \frac{1}{-25} = -\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{25} = \frac{-5 + 3}{75} = -\frac{2}{75} \implies v = -\frac{75}{2} = -37.5\text{ cm}$$

### 37. What is Snell's law of refraction for a given pair of media and a specific wavelength of light?
